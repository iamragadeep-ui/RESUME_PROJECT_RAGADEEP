Delivery and refund policy

1. Delivery delays longer than 48 hours beyond the expected delivery window may qualify for a follow-up review.
2. Orders in the "DELAYED" state with confirmed carrier notices are eligible for customer notification and case creation.
3. Refunds may be approved only when the order remains delayed, the issue is documented, and the customer has not already received the package.
4. High-value orders or repeated delays require manager approval before issuing a refund.
5. Customer communications must reference the carrier update and expected resolution time.
6. All recommendations must cite the order status, tracking evidence, and policy section used.
